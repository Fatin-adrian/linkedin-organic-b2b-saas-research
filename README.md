# LinkedIn Organic Content Strategy Research for B2B SaaS

## Project Overview

This repository organizes research into expert sources, evidence notes, and strategic insights for building a LinkedIn organic content strategy for a B2B SaaS company like 100Hires.

The project focuses on:

- Expert discovery and validation
- LinkedIn evidence capture
- External evidence organization
- Strategic insight synthesis
- Practical content implications for 100Hires

## Research Objective

The goal is to identify credible experts and extract practical patterns that can inform a LinkedIn organic content playbook for 100Hires.

## Key Strategic Themes

- Trust-building beats click-chasing
- LinkedIn content should educate before it sells
- Category clarity shapes content performance
- Positioning and ICP clarity should guide content
- Repeatable content systems beat random posting
- Distribution and repurposing are part of the strategy
- Workflow context should come before automation claims
- Thought leadership should be useful, human, and POV-driven

## Practical Implications for 100Hires

100Hires can use LinkedIn to:

- Educate founders, recruiters, and hiring managers about hiring workflow problems
- Clarify its product category and positioning
- Build trust before pushing demo links
- Turn recruiting pain points into repeatable content pillars
- Create founder-led or expert-led posts
- Repurpose LinkedIn insights into blogs, newsletters, comparison pages, and sales assets
- Avoid generic AI or automation claims without workflow context
