# LinkedIn Organic Content Strategy Research for B2B SaaS

## Project Overview

This repository organizes research into expert sources, evidence notes, and strategic insights for building a LinkedIn organic content strategy for a B2B SaaS company like 100Hires.

The project focuses on:

* Expert discovery and validation
* LinkedIn evidence capture
* External evidence organization
* Strategic insight synthesis
* Practical content implications for 100Hires

## Strategic Takeaways for 100Hires

This research suggests that 100Hires should use LinkedIn as a trust-building and buyer-education channel, not only as a product promotion channel.

Key takeaways:

* The strongest content angle is hiring workflow clarity: pipeline friction, candidate follow-up, recruiter and founder productivity, and practical hiring operations.
* LinkedIn content should educate founders, recruiters, and hiring managers about hiring problems before pushing demo links.
* 100Hires needs clearer category framing so buyers understand whether to think of it as an ATS, recruiting CRM, hiring workflow tool, or another sharper category.
* Founder-led or expert-led posts can help build trust by sharing practical hiring lessons, market observations, and strong but responsible points of view.
* A repeatable weekly content system is more useful than random posting.
* Generic AI or automation claims should be avoided unless they are connected to real hiring workflow pain points.
* Research insights should be repurposed into LinkedIn posts, blog posts, comparison pages, newsletters, and sales enablement assets.

Overall, the research suggests that 100Hires should use LinkedIn to clarify the market problem, educate the buyer, and build trust before asking for conversion.

## Key Strategic Themes

The research synthesis identifies these themes:

* Trust-building beats click-chasing
* LinkedIn content should educate before it sells
* Category clarity shapes content performance
* Positioning and ICP clarity should guide content
* Repeatable content systems beat random posting
* Distribution and repurposing are part of the strategy
* Workflow context should come before automation claims
* Thought leadership should be useful, human, and POV-driven

## Practical Implications for 100Hires

100Hires can use LinkedIn to:

* Educate founders, recruiters, and hiring managers about hiring workflow problems
* Clarify its product category and positioning
* Build trust before pushing demo links
* Turn recruiting pain points into repeatable content pillars
* Create founder-led or expert-led posts
* Repurpose LinkedIn insights into blogs, newsletters, comparison pages, and sales assets
* Avoid generic AI or automation claims without workflow context
