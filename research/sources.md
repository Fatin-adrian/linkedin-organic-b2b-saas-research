# Sources

## Topic

LinkedIn organic content strategy for B2B SaaS.

## Purpose

This file contains the final 10-expert roster draft selected from the candidate validation process. The experts were chosen because their work can help build a practical LinkedIn organic content playbook for a B2B SaaS company like 100Hires.

## Selection Criteria

Experts were selected based on:

- B2B SaaS or B2B tech relevance.
- Visible LinkedIn organic activity or LinkedIn-related thought leadership.
- Practitioner credibility.
- Representative content that can support a practical playbook.
- External evidence outside LinkedIn.
- Usefulness for 100Hires' LinkedIn organic strategy.
