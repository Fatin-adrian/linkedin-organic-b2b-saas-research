# Candidate Discovery Log

## Purpose

Use this file to track potential expert candidates before deciding whether they belong in the final 10-expert roster in `research/sources.md`.

This is a working validation log, not the final source list. Add candidates here first, review their relevance and evidence, then move only verified experts into the final roster.

## Selection Reminder

Topic: **LinkedIn organic content strategy for B2B SaaS**

Prioritize people who actively practice, teach, or demonstrate LinkedIn organic content strategy in a B2B SaaS context. Good candidates may include founders, marketers, operators, advisors, or agency leaders with clear SaaS relevance and visible organic LinkedIn activity.

Do not add a candidate to the final roster until their identity, role, relevance, and recent content are verified with credible links.

5. Collect at least one source link.
6. Decide whether to add to final roster.

Candidates should not be moved to `research/sources.md` until they are verified with credible evidence and relevant recent content.

## Decision Criteria

Add to final roster only if:

- The person is verified with credible links.
- Their role, company, or client context is relevant to B2B SaaS.
- They have relevant recent content about LinkedIn organic strategy, B2B content, founder-led content, SaaS marketing, demand generation, or related organic distribution topics.
- Their content provides teachable patterns that can support later synthesis in `research/insights.md`.

Reject or leave out if:

- The person is too generic for this research topic.
- Their B2B SaaS relevance is unclear.
- Their LinkedIn activity is inactive, inaccessible, or not meaningfully related to organic content strategy.
- Their identity, claims, sources, or examples cannot be verified.
